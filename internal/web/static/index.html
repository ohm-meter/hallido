        Hallido Powered by siraris

        Welcome to Hallido

        Hallido plays radio on your Sonos speakers – as groups, at the push of a button or as a wake-up alarm. Setup takes about two minutes.

        First, Hallido creates a personal access key. Only devices that know this key can operate Hallido.

        Your access key

        This key is shown only now. This browser remembers it. On other phones or computers, enter it once under Speakers → Change API token.

        Keep it safe, for example in your password manager or on paper next to your NAS. Never share it publicly.

        Find speakers

        Hallido looks for Sonos speakers in your home network.

          Hallido is ready

          Next, create your first scene: a radio station on one or more speakers. A schedule then turns a scene into a wake-up alarm (tab “Schedules”).
